# Overstack - Modular AMS Storage Base

A layered storage base for build plates, tools, AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT. The complete arrangement includes five-slot and eight-slot plate layers, single-, double- and triple-drawer layers, and a split device top.

Plate ledges run to integrated rear stops. The low layer has integral front closures. Layers assemble separately and connect with locating feet and removable rear keys.

Overall size: 495 × 287 × 309 mm. The package contains 58 numbered parts, organized by layer, with separate Chinese and English assembly guides. STL units are millimetres. The 3MF files contain assembly geometry only, not sliced printer projects.

This is a digital prototype. All images are CAD renders. Assembly interference, drawer travel and generic PETG/FDM checks have been performed, but physical fit, load capacity and a complete print profile remain unverified. Suggested tags: AMS, AMS HT, storage, drawer, build plate, modular.

All drawers use 2 mm solid floors, with a reinforced left/right joint on the wide single drawer and unchanged slide interfaces. The design is based on practical experience with existing community models.
